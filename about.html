Building the Future of Forms

We believe that creating forms should be simple, intuitive, and accessible to everyone. That's why we built Formly - to empower businesses of all sizes to collect data effortlessly.

Our Mission

To democratize data collection by making form creation accessible, powerful, and delightful for everyone.

Innovation

We continuously push the boundaries of what's possible with form technology, bringing cutting-edge features that solve real business problems.

User-Centric

Every feature we build starts with understanding our users' needs. We design for simplicity without sacrificing powerful functionality.

Trust & Security

We take data protection seriously. Your information and your customers' data are protected with enterprise-grade security measures.

Our Story

Formly was born out of frustration. Our founders, experienced developers and designers, were tired of the complexity and limitations of existing form builders. They envisioned a platform that would make form creation as simple as drag-and-drop, yet powerful enough for enterprise needs.

Since our launch in 2021, we've helped over 10,000 businesses streamline their data collection processes. From startups collecting their first leads to Fortune 500 companies managing complex workflows, Formly has become the trusted choice for modern businesses.

Today, we're proud to be at the forefront of the no-code movement, empowering anyone to create powerful forms without writing a single line of code.
